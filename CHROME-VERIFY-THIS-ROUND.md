# For Claude in Chrome — verify this round

Open **https://magnus-solar-workspace-platform-446354.onhercules.app/**

**Set the browser window to phone width first** (about 390 pixels). Most of this
company works from a phone and every failure so far has been mobile-only.

Work through the list. For each, say **PASS** or **FAIL** and, when it fails, what
you actually saw. Do not guess and do not read code — click.

---

## 1. Mobile navigation — the most important one

Tap every item in the left navigation in turn: Dashboard, My Day, Tasks,
Pipeline, Projects, Design and Engineering, Procurement, Construction, Permits,
Inventory, Manpower and Equipment, Safety, Operations and Maintenance, Documents,
Messages, Finance, Human Resource, Payroll, Reports, Administration.

**Report which ones open and which do not.** A staff member reported that only
Notifications opens on mobile and no other tab does.

## 2. Group controls

Messages → open a group under SPACES → open the group information panel.

- Can you rename the group? Say how you got there.
- Can you remove a member? Say how.
- Are the controls visible without hovering?
- Do any people named `[TEST RECORD]` still appear in the invite list?

## 3. Reply

In a group, reply to an existing message.

- Is there a Reply action on a message, reachable at phone width?
- After sending, does the reply show the message it answers above it?
- Repeat inside a record thread under THREADS.

## 4. Project names

Look at the THREADS section in the Messages sidebar.

Copy the label of a project thread and a site report thread **exactly as
written**. It should read like
`480_kW_Calamba Agro Industrial Corporation_PRJ-2026-0004`, not `PRJ-2026-0004`.

## 5. Direct messages

Does the DIRECT section show the other person's name, or the words
"Direct Message"?

## 6. Attachments

Send a photograph in a group.

- Does it appear as a thumbnail, or only as a download link?
- Open it. Does it display?

## 7. Search

Type a word you know exists in an older message.

- Does anything come back?
- Does it find messages not already visible on screen?

## Report

A numbered list, PASS or FAIL each, one line of what you saw. Screenshot anything
that fails. Do not fix anything — only report.
