## 1. Context

Magnus runs its projects today on Facebook Messenger groups, spreadsheets and shared Google Drive folders. We have built one operations platform to replace all of that: the **Magnus Workspace Platform**, at

**https://magnus-solar-workspace-platform-446354.onhercules.app**

Files uploaded to it land in the company Google Drive automatically. Conversations happen on the record they are about — a project, a purchase order, a site report — so the answer to "what was agreed" stays findable years later.

The platform is being finished module by module. **Live today:** sign-in, people and roles, messaging (the General company space, groups, direct messages, a discussion thread on every project), file attachments, tasks. **Being rebuilt this week:** site reporting, procurement, finance, permits, inventory, payroll. **After that:** loading the company's real records.

Your job: prove the live parts work, own the bug record, make each Messenger move technically sound, and collect the project-side records. You are not expected to fix bugs or build anything — you find, record, coordinate and report.

## 2. What done means for you

1. The testing list in section 4 is complete with every result logged.
2. The Platform Bugs group is running, every defect is logged with severity, and **zero critical bugs remain open**. Critical means: someone cannot sign in, a message or file is lost, someone sees information their role should not allow, or a screen cannot complete its basic job.
3. Every Messenger group move is technically verified — the platform space exists, members can post, attachments reach Drive.
4. Every project-side record sheet in section 6 is collected and checked.

## 3. First day

1. Karl grants your sign-in (Administration → Persons → Grant Sign-In). Sign in, post one message in General.
2. Create one group named **Platform Bugs** with you, Christianah and Karl. You own this group. Every defect anyone finds is one message here: what was done, what was expected, what happened, screenshot, device. Severity on every entry: **Critical** (definitions above) · **Major** (wrong behaviour, but work can continue another way) · **Minor** (wording, layout, abbreviations or ampersands on labels — log these too, the platform's standard is full words, and extra clicks).
3. On a phone, the platform installs like an app: open the address in Chrome, menu, "Add to Home screen." You will later walk every Person In Charge through this — they live on the phone version. Field crew members deliberately have no accounts; do not set any up for them.

## 4. Testing what is live

Spend your first two days using the platform hard, laptop and phone. Work the list, log every result:

1. Sign in and out on laptop and phone; sign in again — nothing lost.
2. Post in General; Christianah confirms she sees it without refreshing.
3. Direct message Christianah; exactly one notification, to her only.
4. Create a group, invite only Christianah; confirm a third person cannot see it exists.
5. Mention her with @ — one notification; post without a mention — none.
6. Open a project from Projects, post on its thread; the same message appears under Messages → Threads.
7. Attach a photograph and a document (under 10 megabytes); download both back and open them; confirm with Karl the files appeared inside the Magnus Workspace folder on the company Google Drive.
8. Convert a message to a task; it appears in My Day with the right assignee.
9. Try to edit or delete a sent message — must not be possible.
10. Type while a dropdown is open, single-click rows, press Escape inside dialogs — log anywhere the screen misbehaves.
11. On the phone: count taps from home screen to a sent message on a project thread. More taps than Messenger is a defect by our own standard — log it.

**Do not test finance, site reports, procurement, inventory or payroll yet.** Those screens are being rebuilt; testing them now reports failures on screens about to be replaced. Karl will hand over separate test packs (Sets A, B and C) when those modules are ready.

## 5. The technical side of each Messenger move

Christianah decides with Karl which group moves when, announces it and moves the people. For every move, you:

1. Confirm the destination exists: a project's talk belongs on that project's own thread; a group is created only for talk with no project behind it.
2. Verify every member can post there, on their own device, before the old group is frozen.
3. Verify one attachment sent in the new space reaches Drive.
4. Log the move: group name, platform space, verified date. Christianah freezes the old group only after your verification.

## 6. Project-side records

You chase and check these sheets: **Projects, Contracts, Project Parties, Project Blocks, Bill of Materials, Open Purchase Orders, Project Permits, Equipment, Opening Stock** (with the three warehouse custodians). You collect and check; loading happens later through separate tools. Track per sheet: owner, agreed date, received date, checked date.
