## This is the live company platform. Read the rules before you touch anything.

Sixty real employees are about to start using this. Their records are already in it.
The company knows testing is happening and will not be confused by records that begin
with `Z2-` — that prefix is the entire safety mechanism, so never skip it.

### Never, under any circumstances

1. **Do not open Administration.** Not one tab. It holds a destructive operation and
   the company's real role assignments. If something you want is only reachable there,
   write down that you could not reach it and move on.
2. **Do not open Migration and Cutover to write anything.** It creates person records

   is not.

   requisitions, no reviews, no payroll periods. Those records are auto-numbered, cannot
   carry your prefix, and cannot be deleted. Both screens were already tested last week.
   **Above all, never approve a statutory rate table** — approving one supersedes the
   previous table and becomes the basis on which real people are paid.
4. **Do not touch anything you did not create.** No renaming, no deleting, no editing,
   no status changes on any record without a `Z2-` prefix.
5. **Do not touch any person.** Do not add or remove anyone from any space, do not
   assign or revoke a role, do not hide or delete anyone's message.
6. **Do not post in General or any company-wide conversation.** Real people read it.
7. **Do not open Hercules.** Not the workspace, not the preview pane, not the chat.
   The company's build credits are exhausted and prompting there costs money. Work only
   in the published application at the address above.

- Prefix everything `Z2-`.
